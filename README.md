---

CIO at **[Bookme](https://bookme.pk)**, Pakistan's largest travel-tech platform, where I've spent the last several years scaling the systems behind millions of bookings. I'm now leading our expansion into Saudi Arabia, South Africa, and the Philippines.

I lead engineering, but I'm still in the codebase. I've personally built our **Amadeus** and **Travelport** GDS integrations, airline direct-connects, and hotel-aggregation pipelines, plus the infrastructure that keeps them fast under real traffic. Background in **infosec** and **infrastructure ops**: I care about systems that stay up and don't fall over at peak.

### What I work on

- ⚙️ **Travel-tech at scale:** GDS, airline, and hotel integrations powering high-volume booking flows
- 💳 **Payments:** multi-currency, multi-gateway processing wired into ~80% of Pakistan's banks
- 🕋 Built Bookme's pipeline as official OTA partner for the **Saudi Ministry of Hajj & Umrah** (online Umrah visa issuance)
- 🚀 **High-traffic systems:** caching, load testing, observability, zero-downtime deploys
- 🌏 Scaling infrastructure across Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, and the Philippines
